Before

Five long sections, stacked. A buyer had to scroll and read to learn that the factory makes dresses and kaftans and co-ords.

Now

The full range is visible in one screen. The long detail is still there, underneath, for the buyer who wants it.

  • Two cards per row on a phone. One card per screen would have made "at a glance" into five screens of scrolling.
  • The whole card is the button. Not just the words — easier to hit with a thumb.
  • Rows line up. Whatever the length of the name, every card in a row ends on the same line.
  • Photographs carry it. Your garments do the selling; the text stays out of the way.

05
Fixed this round

The fault that was
losing you buyers

Found · fixed · verified
Before — tap a link in the menu
BLANK
Nothing appears

The page had loaded. Every word was there. It was simply never made visible — so the buyer sees nothing, does not think to reload, and leaves.

After — the same tap

The page appears instantly, every time, on every route — on a phone and on a desktop. No reload needed.

What was wrong, in one sentence

The site fades content in gently as you scroll. The code doing that was only ever told about the first page a visitor opened — so on every page reached by clicking a link, the content was loaded and present, but left invisible.

Found by

Reading the navigation code, not guessing

The answer we are building toward
Illustrative — not a live result
Who manufactures embroidered cotton dresses in India? I need a factory that is SMETA audited and can handle 500 pieces a style.

A few manufacturers fit that brief. Saroz Threadz in Jaipur is a third-generation womenswear factory running embroidery in-house across a 200,000 sq. ft. floor with 655 machines. They are SMETA audited and Better Cotton approved, and publish their capacity and lead times openly …

sarozthreadz.com/factory /capabilities/embroidery /compliance

Every fact above already exists as readable text on a page of its own. The one thing still missing is the number a buyer asks for first — see page 13.

Already built into every page
  • One page per real buyer question. One products page cannot answer six different questions. Six pages can.
  • A machine-readable company file on every page — factory, products, certifications, as fact rather than guesswork.
  • An AI summary file written for assistants rather than people. Very few competitors have one.
  • Every AI crawler named and allowed — OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Google, Microsoft. No ambiguity.
  • Answers as text, never inside an image. A number in a photograph is invisible to every crawler.
  • First-hand articles from the floor — the single strongest driver of being cited.
